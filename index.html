01About

My research sits at the intersection of efficient AI and intelligent computing systems. I study how to make models reason with less computation, learn from imperfect human signals, and operate reliably under real-world constraints.

I am an Associate Professor at the Global Institute of Future Technology, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, and a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Hong Kong Generative AI Research & Development Center (HKGAI).

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow in Computer Science and Engineering at CUHK. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
